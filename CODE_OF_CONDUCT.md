# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This is a small, focused project about how software works across industries. Everyone is welcome to take part — regardless of experience level, background, identity, or the industry they work in — and everyone should be able to do so without being made to feel unwelcome.

## Expected behaviour

- Be **respectful and constructive**. Critique the content, not the person.
- Assume **good faith**, and ask before assuming someone is wrong.
- Keep discussion **on topic** — this is about the guide, not about winning arguments.
- Accept **corrections gracefully**. Being told a fact is wrong is a normal part of a reference project.
- Respect **privacy and confidentiality** — don't post anyone's private information, and don't paste material you're not allowed to share.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory comments of any kind.
- Personal or political attacks, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Dismissing a correction because of who raised it rather than what it says.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space connected to this project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer, [Sathish R](https://github.com/TechSathish007), privately via GitHub. Reports will be handled discreetly. The maintainer may remove comments, close contributions, or block accounts as needed to keep the project a reasonable place to work.

## Attribution

This code of conduct is a short, project-specific document rather than an adaptation of a standard template.
